names in upper case (reports/models.py: Report and Setup), so a field rename must be
matched in the template.

[Acquisition details (per setup; filled by "Import from NDE")]
BEAM_GAIN            - beam (sum) gain in dB; GAIN is the group gain
BEAM_FORMATION       - Linear / Sectorial / Compound / TOFD / Conventional
ACTIVE_ELEMENTS      - e.g. 1–64
ELEMENT_APERTURE
ELEMENT_STEP
PCS                  - TOFD probe centre separation (in or mm)
SCAN_PATTERN         - e.g. Raster scan
ENCODER_RESOLUTION   - includes units, e.g. Scan 189.97 steps/in; Index 0.5 steps/in
DIGITIZING_FREQUENCY - MHz
PULSE_WIDTH          - ns
BAND_PASS_FILTER     - MHz, e.g. 4–12
GATES                - includes units, e.g. A: 1.29–8.19 µs, 20%
CALIBRATIONS         - calibrations performed, e.g. TCG
SPECIMEN_DIMENSIONS  - includes units, e.g. 11.811 × 11.811 in
SOURCE_FILE          - .nde file name
ACQUISITION_DATE

Numbers are imported without units (the template supplies them, e.g. {{X_RES}}" and
{{FREQ}} MHz); fields marked "includes units" carry their own.
