?
This help
Click
Move the cursors there (S-scan: beam; C-scan: scan + line; B-scan: scan)
Ctrl+click
Reference cursors (red)
Shift+click
Measure cursors (green)
Drag a line
Move that cursor; on the A-scan, drag a gate's bar or its ends
Alt+drag
On the S-scan: move the weld overlay's centre line (Geometry panel to edit its shape)
Select
Click a cursor line, a gate (its bar, edge or letter) or a view (S-scan: the beam cursor; C- / B-scan: the scan cursor); the selection is drawn thicker. Esc deselects
Scroll
Move the selection: a scan line or beam a notch; a cursor or gate about 1/200 of the view (zoom in for finer steps). Shift: 10 times. With nothing selected, zoom at the pointer
Scan / beam / dB
The top bar's boxes: type a value, or scroll over one to step it (scan and beam a line, Shift 10; gain 1 dB, Shift 0.1 dB). The beam box is the angle, the VPA on a linear scan or the index position on a raster
Opening a file
Each open starts as if for the first time: the file's own gates, no cursors, weld or skew changes, and no indications saved from an earlier session (they're deleted). Refreshing the page keeps the current session
Top bar
The selection and where it is: a cursor's value, the beam or scan line, a gate's start (its end: the width; a box's side), following drags and the wheel
Ctrl+scroll
Zoom at the pointer
Right-drag
Pan
Double-click
Reset that view's zoom (the echo curve: the C-scan's)
Palette bar
Right of a thickness / depth C-scan, over the range typed at its top: drag its top down to lower the ceiling, its bottom up to raise the floor, its middle to shift the colours; double-click for the whole range
Zoom bars
Under and left of each view: drag an end of the bar to zoom that axis, its middle to move along it (the A-scan's side bar is the amplitude); double-click a bar to fit that axis
90° / 270°
The S-scan's skew view for angle-beam data (button in its head): 90° - beams top left to bottom right, the index increasing to the right; 270° - top right to bottom left, increasing to the left (the probe, upstream of the weld, reads negative). It starts at the file's skew; the other one mirrors the picture
Rotate S-scan
Display panel: depth across and the index axis down, to line up with the A-scan's depth and a raster C-scan's index axis (link the zooms to keep them together)
Link zoom
Display panel: zoom the scan axis (C ↔ B), depth (B ↔ S ↔ A) or index axis (S ↔ C on a raster) together
Drag a gap
Resize the panels (double-click it to reset)
C-scan
Top view: scan position across, beam / index line down. Click or drag to move the scan and beam cursors; Ctrl / Shift+click put the reference / measure scan cursors. Built from the whole file (again when the gates or gain change)
Echo curve
Under an amplitude C-scan: the gate's highest amplitude along the scan with the sizing drop level; drag its red / green lines to move the ends, click to go to a scan position
B-scan
Side view of the cursor's beam / index line: scan across, depth down. Click to move the scan cursor; Ctrl / Shift+click put the reference / measure scan and depth cursors
S-scan
Click or drag to move the beam cursor. Ctrl / Shift+click put the reference / measure cursors at a point; drag a cursor line or a gate's edge to move it. Dashed lines: the back wall and skips
A-scan
Click: reference cursor, Shift+click: measure cursor. Drag a gate's bar to move it (up / down: its threshold), its end ticks to change its start or width
Gates
SP sound path (an arc on a sectorial scan), TD true depth, Box a box of depth and index. On the S-scan drag a gate's edges, or its letter tab to move it; on the A-scan its bar (up / down: the threshold) and end ticks. Floating: the height follows each A-scan's peak
Panels
Hover a label or button for what it does; click a section's header to fold it (kept)
Eraser
Above each view: clears that view's cursors
